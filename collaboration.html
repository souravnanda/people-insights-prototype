Collaboration and Load

How work actually flows between teams, where it depends on too few people, and which teams are heading toward burnout. Built from calendar and message timing only, never content.

Teams only · no message content · people can opt out
What this dashboard is for
Show how work really flows between teams, where it depends on too few people, and which teams are delivering at the cost of burnout.
Who uses it
HR business partners, team leads and operations leaders
Questions it answers
Where are the bottlenecks? Which teams are overloaded? Who connects teams? Is recognition genuine?
Decisions it supports
Meeting load, staffing, backup owners for fragile links, and fair recognition
Not for
Monitoring individuals or any performance, pay or promotion decision

Suggested actions

Data Platform: protect two meeting-free mornings a week, and move recurring status meetings to written updates.
Support: rotate after-hours cover across a wider group.
Fragile links: name a backup owner for each, and document the knowledge the 2 connectors hold.
Suggestions for team leads and HR business partners. People decide what to do.

Privacy rules

Timing and team only. No message content, meeting titles or documents.
Teams of 5 or more only. Managers never see individual data.
Never used in performance reviews, pay or promotion decisions.
Everyone can see their own data and opt out.
