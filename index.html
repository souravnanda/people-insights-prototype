Retention Radar

An early warning for teams, not individuals. It shows where leaving risk is building, explains why, and suggests what an HR business partner can do before anyone resigns.

No individual scores · groups under 5 hidden
What this dashboard is for
Spot the teams where leaving risk is building, early enough to act, and check whether the action worked.
Who uses it
HR business partners and HR leadership
Questions it answers
Which groups are at risk, and why? What would losing them cost? What should we do?
Decisions it supports
Pay reviews, promotion criteria, manager support, workload and recognition changes
Not for
Scoring, tracking or judging individual employees

Suggested actions for HR business partners
Engineering · mid-level
High

Pay sits below band midpoint for many in this group, and promotions have slowed since the last cycle.

Suggested: run a pay review against band, and publish promotion criteria for the mid-to-senior step.
Customer Support · all
High

Two manager changes in a year, and weekend cover falls on a small part of the team.

Suggested: skip-level check-ins after the reorg, and rebalance the weekend rota.
Suggestions are prompts for a conversation. The HR business partner decides what to do.

What this model will not do

Score or name individuals. Managers never see who might leave.
Use leave patterns, health data, messages or browsing.
Show any group under 5 people.
Act on its own. Every action is chosen by a person.
